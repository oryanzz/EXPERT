# Code of Conduct

EXPERT is a community project for MapleStory players. We want it to be a friendly place for
everyone, whatever their experience, background or the language they write in.

## Our standards

Please:

- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to GitHub or to coding,
- give and take constructive feedback gracefully,
- respect other players' privacy - blur names and chat in screenshots you share.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks,
- sexualized language or imagery,
- publishing other people's private information without permission,
- promoting cheats, hacks, bots or anything that breaks the game's rules.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and
may block people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, contact the maintainer,
**ORYANZ** ([@oryanzz](https://github.com/oryanzz)), on GitHub. Reports are handled discreetly.
